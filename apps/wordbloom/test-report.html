The football club

Coach Fox gives encouragement, a first-letter hint and a second practice attempt. Tests still score the first answer. Writing tests offer player choices immediately; other tests build progress toward a card.

My Team has a 4–3–3 lineup, a bench, a captain and a complete collection. Team data and pending choices survive reloads and full backup restores. Failed saves do not silently consume a player choice.

Limits

There are 32 illustrated portraits and 16 name-and-kit monogram cards. One portrait atlas was rejected by the image tool. Real iPhone/Safari audio and OCR accuracy need a device check. Phone and computer do not automatically synchronise.
